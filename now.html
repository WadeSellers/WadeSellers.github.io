Now

Building

The Burpee Counter just shipped — a fullscreen rep counter for filming a daily 100-burpee video. Yes, there's a streak. Yes, it has a day badge. Yes, it counts by voice, and it's designed to survive the mic mishearing me at rep 70. The quieter obsession is the infrastructure underneath everything: a personal wiki that maintains itself, running on an always-on Mac Mini. That story lives on the AI page now.

Doing

100 burpees. Every day. On camera. It started as a fitness thing and became a shipping-discipline thing: the daily video needed a counter, the counter needed voice, the voice needed to handle failure. Nothing teaches product like being your own most annoyed user seven days a week.

Located

New York City. Specifically: wherever the wifi is decent and the background noise is tolerable, which narrows it down to about four places.

Reading

Whatever's open in the most recent browser tab I didn't mean to leave open. Also some actual books, occasionally.

Thinking about

What one person can ship now that the bottleneck isn't typing speed. The answer keeps moving, and I keep documenting it. Still convinced the play is specificity — small, sharp tools for real people with real problems — and increasingly convinced the second skill is knowing exactly how much to trust the machine, and when.
