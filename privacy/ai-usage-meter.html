Privacy Policy — AI Usage Meter

Last updated: April 7, 2026

AI Usage Meter ("the App") is developed by Wade Sellers. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and your choices.

What the App Does

AI Usage Meter is a macOS menu bar utility that displays your usage quotas for AI services you've connected (e.g., Claude, ChatGPT). It reads usage data directly from these services using your existing login sessions.

Data Collection

Authentication credentials. When you sign in to an AI service through the App, your session cookies or API keys are stored locally on your Mac in the macOS Keychain. These credentials are never transmitted to us or any third party — they are only sent directly to the AI service they belong to.

Usage data. The App fetches your usage/quota information directly from each AI service's API. This data is displayed locally and is never stored, logged, or transmitted beyond your device.

Feedback form. If you choose to send feedback through the App, the following is submitted to Formspree (our form processing service): your feedback message and category, your email address (only if you provide it), and basic device info (macOS version, app version, and number of connected services). We use this solely to read and respond to your feedback. Formspree's privacy policy applies to data processed through their service.

Analytics. The App does not include any analytics, tracking, or telemetry frameworks. We do not collect crash reports, usage patterns, or behavioral data.

Data Storage

All credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain, encrypted by the operating system. App preferences (display settings, refresh interval) are stored in standard macOS UserDefaults. No data is stored on external servers.

Third-Party Services

The App communicates only with AI service APIs (e.g., claude.ai, chatgpt.com) to fetch your usage data using your credentials, and with Formspree (formspree.io) only when you submit the feedback form.

Your Choices

You can disconnect any service at any time from Settings, which removes its stored credentials. You can delete the App at any time; Keychain items are removed with a standard app uninstall. Feedback submission is entirely optional.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach out through the in-app feedback form or email wade@wadesellers.com.
