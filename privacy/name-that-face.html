Privacy Policy — Name That Face

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Name That Face ("the App") is developed by Wade Sellers. The short version: there are no accounts, no tracking, and no ads. Everything you put into the App stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share a deck with a code.

What the App Does

The App turns a PDF of headshots into flashcards so you can learn a group of people's names. It finds the faces and reads the printed names using Apple's Vision and PDFKit frameworks, which run entirely on your device.

Data Collection

The App has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising frameworks. It does not ask you to create an account, and it has no login.

The App only contacts a server when you share a deck with a code or enter a code someone gave you. Nothing is sent otherwise.

Sharing a Deck With a Code

If you tap Share on a deck, that deck — its portraits and names — is uploaded to a server we run so that someone who types the 6-digit code can download it. The shared copy is not tied to your name, email, or any account, because the App has none.

A shared deck is deleted automatically after 30 days, or immediately when you tap Stop Sharing. Anyone who already added the deck keeps their copy on their own phone.

To limit people guessing codes, the server counts wrong codes for each phone (using a random identifier the App creates, which is not linked to you) and for each network address. Those counts are discarded within an hour.

If the portraits are of other people — students, for example — please share only with people who should have them.

What Stays on Your Device

Photos and names. The PDFs you import, the portraits extracted from them, any photos you add from your camera or photo library, and the names attached to them are stored in the App's own storage on your iPhone. They leave your device only if you share a deck with a code, as described above.

Your progress. Which people are in rotation and how well you know each one is stored the same way.

One preference. The App records whether it has already seeded its sample deck of US Presidents, so it doesn't add it twice.

Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to add someone by taking a photo or picking one from your library, iOS will ask your permission first. Those images are used only to make that person's flashcard and are stored on your device like everything else. The App does not read your photo library on its own.

Face Detection

The App detects that a face is present in an image in order to crop a portrait out of a page. It does not identify anyone, does not build a faceprint, does not match faces against any database, and does not use Face ID. All detection happens on your device and the results are not retained beyond the cropped picture you can see.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third Parties

The App includes no third-party SDKs. Shared decks are stored on Cloudflare, which hosts our server; Cloudflare stores them on our behalf and does not use them for anything else. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Your Control and Deletion

You can delete any person or any deck inside the App at any time. Deleting the App from your iPhone removes everything it stored on your phone. Shared decks are deleted when you tap Stop Sharing or after 30 days, whichever comes first. Because shared decks aren't linked to you, we can't look them up by person; if you need one removed and no longer have the App, email us the code.

One thing worth knowing: if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup is made by iOS and can include the App's stored photos and names, the same as any other app. Those backups are governed by Apple's terms, not by us, and we cannot see them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
