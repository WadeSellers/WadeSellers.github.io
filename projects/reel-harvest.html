reel-harvest

AI Pipeline Python Whisper Claude

Share an Instagram Reel from my phone. Ninety seconds later my phone buzzes with a designed PDF: that Reel's proven format, torn down beat by beat and rebuilt as a post script in my own voice, ready to film. Nobody touches a computer.

Under the hood: a Telegram bot hands the link to a daemon on a Mac Mini, which downloads the video, transcribes the spoken words locally with Whisper, deletes the video, and passes the transcript to a headless Claude session. Claude mines the source's beat structure — hook, open loop, payload, button — then rebuilds it out of my material and writes four coordinated artifacts: a grid cover, a two-second hook, a beat-table script, and an algorithm-first caption. Styled HTML becomes a PDF, and the PDF lands back in the same chat.

The two design constraints that shaped everything: $0 marginal cost (local transcription, no metered APIs) and zero babysitting (one share-sheet tap, self-healing daemons, errors retry themselves). It has run unattended since June 2026.
