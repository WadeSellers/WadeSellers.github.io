Names & Faces

iOS SwiftUI Vision On-device AI

Meet 23 strangers on Monday. Greet every one of them by name on Friday. I work at a theater conservatory, and a few times a year a new cohort arrives as a PDF of headshots with names printed underneath — wonderful people, total strangers. There is nothing warmer than someone who's met you exactly once saying “Morning, Coraline” like it was nothing.

You import the PDF. That's the whole workflow. On-device, in seconds, the app renders every page, finds every face, reads the name printed under it, and deals a deck of flashcards. When one headshot defeated Apple's face detector, the app noticed an unclaimed name sitting on the page, figured the photo had to be right above it, and went and got it anyway.

The deck paces itself: you start with three faces, add more when you're feeling dangerous, and the cards quietly show you the people you blank on more often than the ones you know. Everything runs on the phone — no servers, no accounts, the students' photos never leave the device. Built in two days, in conversation with Claude.
