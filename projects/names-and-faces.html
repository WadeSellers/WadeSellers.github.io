Name That Face

iOS SwiftUI Vision On-Device

A few times a year a new cohort walks into the conservatory and somebody hands me a PDF of headshots. Wonderful people. Total strangers. And there is nothing warmer than someone who met you once saying "Morning, Coraline" like it was nothing.

You import the PDF. That's the whole workflow. Every face found, every name read, a deck dealt — on your phone, in seconds.

The group, and the home screen.

You start with three faces, not twenty-three. Add one when you're feeling dangerous. Faces you keep nailing come around less; blank on someone and they come back more. You never manage any of this. The deck pays attention.

All on-device. No servers, no accounts, no "we value your privacy" theater — the students' photos never leave the phone. No PDF handy? It deals you the US Presidents, so you can finally learn which one is Chester A. Arthur.
