Budgeteer

Audio App Finance

Budgeteer is for people who know they should track their spending but find every budgeting app inexplicably annoying. Instead of forms and categories and color-coded pie charts, you just talk to it. Hold the mic, say "twenty-four fifty for lunch", done — AI pulls out the amount and the what, and it's on the board.

The board is the point: a calendar heatmap of your month where every day earns its color. Under budget, green. Getting loose, yellow. You know. Underneath, the two numbers that actually matter — what's left this month, and what you can spend per day without wrecking it.

Voice-first, stays out of your way, designed for the specific person who is great at earning money and terrible at knowing where it goes.
