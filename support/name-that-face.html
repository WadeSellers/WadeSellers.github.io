Name That Face — Support

iOS 18+ iPhone On-Device

Name That Face turns a PDF of headshots into a deck of flashcards, so you can walk into a room of strangers on Monday and greet all of them by name on Friday. Everything happens on your phone.

Something not working, or an idea for it? Email wade@wadesellers.com and you'll get a reply from an actual person.

Getting started

Tap New Deck at the bottom of your list and choose Import a PDF. Pick a file of headshots with names printed near them. The app renders every page, finds every face, reads the name next to it, and hands you a review screen. Check the names, fix anything it misread, then add everyone.

No PDF yet? The app comes with a sample deck of US Presidents so you can try the game first.

How studying works

You start with three faces, not the whole group. Say the name out loud, tap the card to check, then swipe right if you knew it and left if you didn't. When you're ready for more people, tap Add Face. A new face comes around often; once you keep getting someone right, they come around less. Blank on someone you thought you knew and they'll start showing up more again. You don't manage any of this.

What kind of PDF works?

Anything where each person's name is printed near their photo — below it, above it, or beside it. Class rosters, cast lists, yearbook pages, staff directories and conference programs all work. Small type is fine.

It needs a real PDF, not a photo of a printed page. If your sheet is on paper, scan it to PDF first — the Files app on iPhone can do this from the camera.

A name came out wrong

Tap the name on the review screen and edit it. The field grows so you can see the whole thing, and Done finishes the edit. You can also fix a name later: tap the person in their deck and edit them there.

Part of the printed name is showing in the photo

Tap the photo — on the review screen or on a person — and the cropper opens. Trim it out and tap Done. The original is kept, so you can always reset a crop or redo it.

Somebody got missed

If a face didn't come through, add them by hand: open the deck, tap +, then Add Person, and take a photo or choose one from your library.

Can I remove someone, or start over?

Swipe to delete a deck from the home screen. Inside a deck, tap a person and choose Delete Person. To keep a deck but wipe your progress, use Reset Progress in the deck's menu.

How do I share a deck?

Open the deck and tap the share button. You'll get a 6-digit code — tap Copy Code, or read it out. The other person taps New Deck, chooses Enter a Code, and types it. They get the whole deck, cropped and named.

Codes work for 30 days. To turn one off sooner, tap share again and choose Stop Sharing. Anyone who already added the deck keeps it.

It says my code didn't work

Check the digits, and check that it hasn't been more than 30 days. After 10 wrong codes, a phone has to wait an hour before trying again.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no accounts and no analytics, and the faces you import stay on your iPhone. The only time anything leaves it is when you share a deck with a code: that deck is stored online for up to 30 days so the code can find it, and it isn't linked to you. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

If those headshots are students, share the code only with people who should have their photos.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not yet. Decks live on the iPhone you made them on.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
